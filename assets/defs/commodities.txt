# World commodity markets besides crude oil (hand-written; moddable). Crude oil keeps
# its own market in src/sim/systems/markets.cpp (OPEC+ decisions); these markets run in
# src/sim/systems/commodities.cpp with the same stock-flow price rule:
#     target = price0 * exp(-flow_sensitivity * (supply - demand)/demand0
#                           - inventory_sensitivity * (stocks - normal)/(demand0 * inventory_days))
# Prices are indexes (1 = 2019 average). Producer and consumer shares per nation are in
# assets/scenario/<year>/commodities_curated.csv.
#
#  world_value_bn      2019 world production value, USD bn (gas: ~4,000 bcm; coal: ~8 Gt;
#                      grains: wheat + maize + rice ~2.7 Gt; metals: iron ore 2.4 Gt + copper
#                      20 Mt). BP Statistical Review 2020, FAOSTAT, USGS; rounded.
#  demand_elasticity   short-run price elasticity of demand (Labandeira, Labeaga & Lopez-Otero
#                      2017 meta-analysis for energy; Roberts & Schlenker 2013 for grains)
#  supply_elasticity   short-run price elasticity of supply
#  inventory_days      normal stocks in days of demand (grain stocks-to-use ~30%)
#  harvest_sd          quarterly s.d. of the supply shock (weather)
#  biofuel_share       demand share that tracks the crude price (maize ethanol, vegetable oils)
#  income_elasticity   demand response to household income (Engel's law: food low)
#  weights{ sector{w} }  demand follows the activity of these sectors; without weights,
#                      demand follows population and income.

commodity{ key{gas}  name{Natural gas}  world_value_bn{800} demand_elasticity{-0.10} supply_elasticity{0.05}
           inventory_days{60} flow_sensitivity{3.0} inventory_sensitivity{1.5} price_speed{0.05}
           weights{ ene{0.45} man{0.35} csv{0.10} gov{0.10} } }
