# Technologies that diffuse across nations (hand-written; moddable). Read by
# src/sim/scenario_ext.cpp; used by src/sim/systems/longcampaign.cpp.
#
# Adoption follows a logistic curve (Griliches 1957): a(t) = 1 / (1 + exp(-speed (t - t50))).
# The frontier reaches half adoption in `frontier_year`; every other nation lags by
# lag_years plus an income, skills and openness gap to the frontier (Comin & Hobijn 2010:
# adoption lags shrink for newer technologies but stay large across income levels).
#
# Effects scale with adoption relative to the 2020 start (the calibrated baseline):
#  tfp / sector_gain   productivity of one sector (value added per unit of capacity)
#  oil_cut             share of oil demand removed at full adoption
#  emission_cut        share of CO2 per unit of oil-equivalent energy removed at full adoption
#  connectivity_gain   rise in social-media connectivity at full adoption
#  vaccine_speedup     share of vaccine development time removed at full frontier adoption
#
# Calibration notes (rounded): EVs were 2.6% of world car sales in 2019 (IEA Global EV
# Outlook 2020) - half of sales near 2030 at the frontier; renewables (IEA World Energy
# Outlook 2020, IRENA); AI services (frontier mid-2030s; productivity effects modest:
# Brynjolfsson, Rock & Syverson 2021 J-curve); mRNA platform vaccines (first licensed 2020).
