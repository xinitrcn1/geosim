# Pathogens (hand-written; moddable). Every pathogen runs its own two-age-group
# SEIR on the same population; they interact through shared hospital capacity,
# cross-immunity, deaths, and the behavioural / policy response to the combined
# burden. Scenario events seed them: epidemic_seed{ ... pathogen{key} ... }.
#
# key               up to 4 characters
# r0                basic reproduction number
# incubation_days   mean latent period;  infectious_days: mean infectious period
# death_delay_days  end of infectiousness to death
# ifr_young/old     infection fatality rate under / over 65
# old_contact_factor relative contact rate of the 65+ group
# seasonality       amplitude of seasonal forcing (sign by hemisphere)
# waning_days       mean duration of immunity (0 = lifelong for the scenario)
# hosp_share        share of infectious needing a hospital bed
# detection_ratio   share of infections detected at maximum testing
# npi_sensitivity   how much NPIs cut transmission (1 = respiratory; ~0.2 vector-borne)
# initial_immunity  share of the population immune at the scenario start
# cross_immunity    susceptibility removed by having recovered from another pathogen{ key{share} }

# SARS-CoV-2, wild type. Flaxman et al. 2020 (R0); Levin et al. 2020 (IFR by age, rounded to two groups).
#
# vaccine_days       days from first sighting to the first vaccine (0 = none); SARS-CoV-2: the
#                    first emergency use authorisation (2020-12-11) ~345 days after the outbreak
# vaccine_efficacy   share of vaccinated susceptibles made immune
# vaccine_ramp_days  days for world production to reach vaccine_world_capacity
# emergent{1}        a template that can emerge at random (pandemic_rate); not seeded otherwise
pathogen{
	key{cov}
	name{SARS-CoV-2 (wild type)}
	r0{3.0} incubation_days{4} infectious_days{5} death_delay_days{14}
	ifr_young{0.0015} ifr_old{0.045} old_contact_factor{0.6}
	seasonality{0.25} waning_days{0} hosp_share{0.05} detection_ratio{0.15}
	npi_sensitivity{1.0} initial_immunity{0.0}
	vaccine_days{345} vaccine_efficacy{0.9} vaccine_ramp_days{270}
}

# Seasonal influenza: R0 ~1.3 (Biggerstaff et al. 2014), IFR ~0.01-0.05% overall, strong
# seasonality, a third of the population immune at the start of a season. Nearly vanished
# after March 2020 under COVID measures (WHO FluNet), which the back-test can check.
pathogen{
	key{flu}
	name{Seasonal influenza}
	r0{1.4} incubation_days{2} infectious_days{3} death_delay_days{10}
	ifr_young{0.00005} ifr_old{0.002} old_contact_factor{0.8}
	seasonality{0.4} waning_days{365} hosp_share{0.01} detection_ratio{0.05}
	npi_sensitivity{1.0} initial_immunity{0.35}
	cross_immunity{ cov{0.0} }
}
