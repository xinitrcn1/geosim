# key           matches assets/scenario/<year>/sectors.csv
# role          generic | government | energy | contact | manufacturing (see src/sim/model.hpp)
# labor_share   compensation of employees / value added (OECD national accounts, rounded)
# oil_intensity relative oil use per unit of output (transport-heavy services highest)
# import_intensity relative import content per unit of output (goods >> services; OECD TiVA, rounded)
# voluntary_distancing  1 = demand falls when people avoid contact (Goolsbee & Syverson 2021)
# demand        prior share of each final-demand component (hh, gov, inv, exp) landing in this
#               sector. The loader rescales them (RAS) so baseline sector value added matches data.
# skills        employment mix by skill tier (low, mid, high); ILO/OECD occupation data, rounded
# npi_capacity  capacity lost at the maximum level of each OxCGRT indicator (summed, capped at 95%).
#               Contact services lose ~58% at every indicator's maximum, in line with the deepest
#               observed 2020 hospitality/retail/transport output falls.
#               Informed by Dingel & Neiman (2020) teleworkability and del Rio-Chanona et al. (2020)
#               supply-shock estimates by industry.
